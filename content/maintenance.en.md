# Oiling
- In heavy-use environments, apply blade oil **once every two weeks**.
- Apply a thin line of shredder oil, run reverse for 5 seconds, then forward for 10 seconds.

# Jam Response
- Reduce sheet input immediately and run reverse 2-3 times.
- If a jam continues, power off, remove scraps with tweezers, then restart.

# Thermal Management
- Do not exceed the continuous runtime (`run_time_min`).
- If thermal protection is triggered, cool down for at least 20 minutes.

# Lifespan Tips
- Remove staples, clips, and labels before feeding.
- Emptying the bin is a good time to clean dust around the cutting head.

# FAQ
## Q. Can I feed 2-3 sheets above the rating?
A. It may still run temporarily, but motor load rises sharply and long-term wear increases.

## Q. The noise suddenly got much louder.
A. This usually means missed oiling intervals or foreign material in the blades. If noise remains after oiling, inspect the unit.
